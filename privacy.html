Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

CS_ata rclone is a personal-use application used by the project owner to back up and synchronize CS_ata project data with the owner's own Google Drive account.

Google User Data

The application accesses Google Drive only for the purpose of creating, reading, updating, and managing files required for the owner's backup and synchronization workflow.

Use of Data

Google user data is used only to operate the personal backup and synchronization functions of CS_ata.

Storage of Credentials

OAuth credentials and authorization tokens are stored on systems controlled by the application owner and are used only to authenticate access to the owner's Google Drive.

Sharing of Data

Google user data is not sold, rented, transferred, or shared with third parties for advertising, analytics, or other commercial purposes.

Data Retention

Backup files remain in the owner's Google Drive until the owner deletes them according to the project's backup and retention procedures.

Access Revocation

The owner may revoke the application's Google account access at any time through their Google Account security settings.
